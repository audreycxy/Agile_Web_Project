How it works

Simple to start, fun to improve.

1. Create an account

Sign up as a player so your game results can be saved between sessions.

2. Click the egg

Play the clicking game and try to beat your previous score.

3. Track progress

View your score history and check whether your performance improves.

Game features

A complete role-based game system.

Player mode

Players can log in, play the game, save scores, and view their history.

Guest mode

Guests can try the game quickly without creating a permanent account.

Admin view

Admin users can view player accounts and game result information.
